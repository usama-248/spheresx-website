When Flutter fits your product

Practical guidance for founders and teams planning their next digital product.

SpheresX Journal · 1 min read

Look at the platforms you need

Flutter can support a shared application codebase across Android and iOS. The product still needs attention to platform-specific permissions, integrations and store requirements.

Check the difficult integrations early

Prototype camera, Bluetooth, payments, background work or device connections before committing to a broad feature plan. Those integrations often shape implementation decisions.

Think beyond the first release

A clear architecture and deliberate state-management approach help the app remain understandable as the product grows. Plan maintenance alongside development.
